Learning project

Market Microstructure Simulator

A small crypto market simulator built step by step to understand orders, matching, the limit order book, recording, and midprice movement.

Open dashboard View repo
What it shows

Orders moving through the market system

  1. Random agents create synthetic orders.
  2. The exchange validates and assigns IDs.
  3. The matching engine applies price-time priority.
  4. The order book updates resting liquidity.
  5. The recorder stores trades, snapshots, and midprice.
